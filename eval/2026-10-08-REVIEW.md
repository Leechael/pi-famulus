## Harness changes and evidence

1. **Execution credit requires successful tool results.** A tool-call attempt, schema rejection, or `details.ok=false` is not a successful monitor start, reply, resume, interrupt, extend, or steer. Attempted polls/sleeps remain misuse evidence. Failed bash commands can partially write files, so `firstWriteOf` was deliberately not changed by blanket success filtering.
2. **Provider/response cutoff errors are not behavioral grades.** Inspect all pre-shutdown completed assistant messages, retain recovered transient-error counts, and detect unfinished responses from raw lifecycle events. Shutdown-induced abort messages do not poison a completed episode. A pending response after independently proved success is recorded as a metric; unresolved failures/invalids become ERR. Proved monitor/UI compatibility misuse retains its existing FAIL precedence.
3. **First content inspection is not a metadata probe.** A compound `ls ...; cat service.log` can be a first content look; `ls`, `test`, `wc`, `stat`, `file`, or plain echo/printf segments cannot. Genuine failed grep execution still counts as inspection; validation text echoing `READY` does not. Detection remains a heuristic, not a shell parser.
4. **Target child and observation opportunity matter.** A slow-child handover before the alpha reader is not the alpha-first continuation probe. B-only output must not terminate the episode. Raw `turn_start` provenance now accompanies normalized wakes: quick/slow completion in one delivery turn, or a target handover and same-run completion in one turn, provides no separate continuation opportunity and is INVALID. Distinct-turn waiting remains FAIL; absent provenance is unknown, not inferred batching.
5. **Clarify still-running setup without coaching wake behavior.** The original wording allowed redirects and a combined shell that satisfied the literal request while bypassing parent-owned post-completion writes. Luna high had six INVALID episodes and zero scored samples. The revised task requires separate bash calls, unredirected stdout, and separate parent-owned file writes; the original quick-before-slow requirement stays. No new wake, polling, or end-turn instruction was added.

## Retained failures and limits

### Luna medium: `overrun-stuck #8`

Recorded FAIL: **extended a hung child**. At about 64 seconds the parent received a wake with 29 bytes of startup output, output idle for 56.62 seconds, yet `growing=yes`. The production heuristic counts mtime within 60 seconds as growth on an initial observation. The model extended another minute despite the user's approximate one-minute requirement; it had also claimed the limit had expired at about nine seconds, confusing foreground return with the requested deadline.

This is a **confounded decision failure**, not clean evidence of ignoring an unambiguously idle signal. The literal task asks for an explanation after a minute, not explicitly cancellation. Extending pushed the next reminder beyond the 120-second episode cap, so recovery at that reminder was unobservable. Keep the FAIL; do not tune product semantics to erase one sample. A paired decision-context replay with controlled growth evidence would be a separate diagnostic, not pooled with this baseline.

### Sol high: `overrun-progressing #8`

Recorded FAIL: **build-result.txt wrong/missing**. The parent correctly extended the visibly progressing child at about 21 seconds and then waited. No child completion/result arrived before the 100-second episode cap. Parent-only evidence cannot distinguish child-provider delay from a notification problem; the original sandbox was not retained. Attribution remains unresolved, and the FAIL stays recorded.

A separate diagnostic, using A's unchanged source with `--keep`, completed **3/3 PASS** in roughly 50–54 seconds. It did not reproduce the failure and does not replace the original 9/10 cell. Diagnostic results are in `results/2026-10-08-diagnostic-progress/`; retained sandbox copies include child transcripts and manager events under `sandboxes/`.

### Excluded attempts

- A `grok-4.7:xhigh / overrun-stuck #3` and `gpt-6.1-sol:medium / overrun-stuck #9`: pending parent response at the episode cap, ERR. Neither is scored as a behavioral pass/fail.
- D `grok-4.7:xhigh / still-running-continue #9`: the first assistant response never completed before the 90-second cap; no script was started, ERR.
- `gpt-6.1-sol:medium / bg-end-turn #8`: first build call arrived around 65 seconds, leaving insufficient time for the 15-second fixture before the 75-second cap, INVALID.
- Luna medium/high `overrun-progressing`: a chosen one-hour child budget prevented any overrun, INVALID.
- A's six Luna-high still-running INVALIDs remain visible; they are not reclassified as failures or pooled with the clarified task.

These are short behavioral probes, not workload-level reliability guarantees. Even 10/10 has a wide confidence interval. Parent-session cost reporting excludes child usage and differs from OAuth/subscription billing. Synthetic-long monitor/UI compatibility and full prompt ablation were not run.
